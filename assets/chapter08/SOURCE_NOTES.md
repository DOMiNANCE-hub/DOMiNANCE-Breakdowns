## Physiological clarifications
- Direct Fick calculation uses oxygen *content* rather than saturation alone, because haemoglobin concentration matters.
- Mixed venous blood from a pulmonary-artery catheter approximates whole-body venous return better than a regional vein sample.
- An assumed O₂ consumption can introduce measurement error; rapidly changing physiology violates steady-state assumptions.
- The lecturer's 'work = stroke volume × pressure' is a useful approximation, not an exact total myocardial energy equation. Stroke-work area includes net ventricular pressure–volume work and differs from total myocardial oxygen use.
- Increased pressure loading can elevate myocardial oxygen demand, but the lecture's aortic stenosis/regurgitation angina comparison should not be treated as a universal rule.
- Right and left ventricles pump similar volumes per beat over time but normally eject against very different arterial pressures.
