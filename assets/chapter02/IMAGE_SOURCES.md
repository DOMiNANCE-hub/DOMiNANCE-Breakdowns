# Chapter 02 — Selected teaching figures

These are the three selected source images for the approved **Cardiac Muscle Physiology** chapter. They are **external images**; the binary image files are **not yet committed** to this repository. Include them where the relevant mechanism is taught, with captions and attribution.

## Figure 01 — Cardiac muscle and intercalated discs
- Section: 2. Intercalated discs
- Direct image: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/7/7e/1020_Cardiac_Muscle.jpg
- Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:1020_Cardiac_Muscle.jpg
- Credit: OpenStax, *Anatomy & Physiology*, "1020 Cardiac Muscle"

## Figure 03 — Calcium cycling and excitation–contraction coupling
