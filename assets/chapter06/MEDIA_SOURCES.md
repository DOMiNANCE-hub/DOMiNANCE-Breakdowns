# Chapter 06 — Electrocardiography: visual sources and rights

## Primary teaching source
- **Lecture 7 ECG 2.pdf**, slides 1–14: definitions, limb and chest leads, P/QRS/T/U waves, ST and PR intervals, QT interval, calibration and rate calculations.
- Lecture slide 14 contains visibly unequal R–R intervals. Its example 1500 ÷ 22 yields an interval-specific estimate, not automatically the average of an irregular tracing.
- Basic clinical rhythm recognition and AV block patterns are **supplementary** to the primary slide scope.

## Compact calibration grid
- Original CSS-rendered square grid, not copied from the lecture.
- On a standard ECG recording, at 25 mm/s, small horizontal square = 0.04 seconds; at 10 mm/mV, small vertical square = 0.1 mV.
- Illustrative only; does not simulate a diagnostic trace.
