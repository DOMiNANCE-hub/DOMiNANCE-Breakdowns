## Important scope limitations

1. This is an academic **coverage mapping and explanatory-text enhancement**, not a literal transcription of slide decks. Lecture-specific figures are not embedded without reuse rights; conceptually equivalent original diagrams or prose are used.
2. The uploaded set does **not** supply a standalone detailed cardiac-muscle lecture. The muscle, action-potential and excitation–contraction additions are explicitly supplementary physiology material.
3. Scientific corrections are deliberately distinguished from lecturer wording. For disputed numerical reference ranges, use the lecture values as historical teaching context and identify values that are condition-dependent or no longer generally accepted.
4. A final pre-release quality test should still compare *rendered website diagrams* with their labelled source concepts and check mobile display. Mapping every slide does not replace proof-reading figures visually.
5. The material supports undergraduate preclinical learning and is not a clinical decision guide.
